# AGENTS.md

This repository is the user's core knowledge base. Treat its content as long-term knowledge accumulation rather than isolated article production.

## Learning and Writing Preference

- Prefer top-down explanations: begin with the high-level idea, motivation, and evolution of the problem and solution before discussing implementation details.
- Connect general principles to concrete mechanisms, implementations, tradeoffs, and failure modes.
- Preserve the reasoning path that led to the conclusion, especially important conceptual shifts and rejected alternatives.
- Optimize notes for building reusable intuition and a deeper understanding of the underlying ideas, not just memorizing a final implementation.
- A useful default structure is: high-level idea and motivation → evolution of the thinking → concrete mechanisms and implementation → tradeoffs and failure modes → reusable principles.
